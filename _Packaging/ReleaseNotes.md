### Added
- Zul'jarra's Forces Champion added to The Coiled Isle reputation achievements
- Captain Tokka's Crew now shows reputation progress, which Blizzard leaves out of the achievement itself
- Season limited achievements can now be narrowed down to specific weeks of a season: Fabled Let Me Solo Him: Azta'rec is marked as obtainable in week 1 of Midnight Season 2 only
- Temporarily obtainable tooltips now show a season's start and end date next to the season name (Options -> Layout -> Tooltip - Temporarily obtainable -> Show Start Time & End Time)

### Fixed
- Custom criteria, the extra objectives progress the addon adds for achievements Blizzard leaves blank, only showed up on achievement links in chat and were missing from the achievement list, the achievement tooltip and search (dev note: they used to be applied by overriding `GetAchievementCriteriaInfo` and `GetAchievementNumCriteria` globally. That was removed in 93.8 because it tainted everything reading those functions, but nothing replaced it, leaving the whole feature inert. The addon's own rendering paths now look up custom criteria directly and fall back to the game's API, so no global is touched)
- Season limited achievements showed the wrong obtainable state around a season boundary, up to two weeks either side of it (dev note: `C_MythicPlus.GetCurrentSeason` and `GetCurrentArenaSeason` report a season up to two weeks before it opens and keep reporting it up to two weeks after it closed. Season start and end dates are now registered per client through the new `KrowiAF.AddSeasonData` API and take priority over the game's own season number. An anchor is a region agnostic `{year, month, day}` marker holding the date Blizzard announced for NA, which each client snaps to its own weekly reset, so the exact instant stays correct per region and across DST)

### Fixed (100.2)
- Fixed several PvP achievements not being recognized by the PvP filter (Tour of Duty and Training Grounds achievements)

### Added (100.3)
- Winds of Mysterious Fortune added to the Events tab under Holidays, with the Winds of Particularly Mysterious Fortune feat of strength for the September 2026 occurrence (dev note: the achievement is a server side hotfix, so it is in no client build and none of the lookup scripts can see it. Id 64140, its name, description and the 500 Trader's Tender reward were read out of the live client's own hotfix cache, `_retail_/Cache/ADB/enUS/DBCache.bin*`, as the record with recordId 64140 under Achievement.db2's table hash 0xD2EE2CA7; that hash was confirmed by cross checking four other record ids in the same cache against the game DB. The event is region split over five Holidays.db2 rows, 1636 us, 1670 eu, 1671 kr, 1672 tw and 1683 cn, and all five are listed so the reminder and the obtainable state resolve in every region. The feat is gated with `Obtainable("Event", 1636)`, so its obtainable state follows the event's own calendar occurrence per region rather than a hardcoded date)
- Map Verifier (debug tool): one Export and one Import button replace the six partial exports. Export writes the whole verifier state (verdicts, link groups, expansions, overrides, comments) as a single CSV; Import validates a pasted CSV, shows what would change and replaces the saved data after confirmation

### Fixed (100.3)
- Zone achievements (Current Zone category and the World Map button) now follow the game's own criteria: meta achievements such as The Fires of Azeroth, Tricks and Treats of Azeroth, To Honor One's Elders, Glory of the Legion Hero, the Midnight Pathfinder and the Battle for Azeroth war campaigns appear on every zone where one of their parts can be progressed, the Pet Mauler achievements appear on all 67 zones their criteria name (Cataclysm and Pandaria zones were missing), Elders of Eastern Kingdoms appears in The Hinterlands, and You're So Offensive no longer appears in Magisters' Terrace. Meta achievements that were on no zone at all now appear where their parts are earned: Dungeonmaster, Dungeon Hero and Glory metas on every dungeon of their expansion, Classic and Outland Raider on their raids, Battlemaster and Khan on their battlegrounds, Hallowed Be Thy Name, The Flame Warden and Keeper, Merrymaker, Brewmaster and What a Long, Strange Trip It's Been on the zones, capitals and holiday-boss dungeons of their events, the Battle for Azeroth, Legion, War Within and Shadowlands expansion metas on their zones, dungeons and raids, and the Khaz Algar Completionist, Glyph Hunter, Explorer and Loremaster metas on the four Khaz Algar zones. Dragonflight zones, dungeons, Valdrakken, the Forbidden Reach, Zaralek Cavern and the Emerald Dream now have proper zone lists (they were served by the old fallback table and lacked the meta achievements). Taking the Show on the Road and Field Photographer show in the capitals they are performed in, Explore Tanaris in the Caverns of Time, Explore Voidstorm on Slayer's Rise, the Shadowlands explore achievements in the covenant sanctums, Welcome to Draenor on the intro Tanaan Jungle, Exile's Reach on Exile's Reach and Dracthyr, Awaken on the Forbidden Reach. Season-wide Mythic+ achievements (Keystone Explorer, Conqueror, Master, Hero, Legend and Myth of a season, the season titles and the Resilient Keystone series) now appear in every dungeon of that season's pool for Shadowlands Seasons 3 and 4, all Dragonflight, War Within and Midnight seasons; the four season-independent Legion keystone achievements (Keystone Initiate, Challenger, Conqueror, Master) no longer appear in the Shadowlands dungeons, since any keystone dungeon of any season counts for them. Continent maps (Kalimdor, Eastern Kingdoms, Outland, Northrend, Pandaria, Draenor, Broken Isles, Zandalar, Kul Tiras, The Shadowlands, Quel'Thalas) no longer list achievements: a player is never "in" a continent, so those lists only duplicated the zones (dev note: the World Map button aggregates the zones of a continent at runtime instead, see the next entry. Placement is verified offline against the achievement criteria trees by `raw/Evaluate-ZoneCriteria.ps1`; the rules are in `.claude/skills/add-zone-data/SKILL.md` and the decision record in `wiki/achievement-data/zone-data-format.md`)
- The World Map button on a continent map (Kalimdor, Eastern Kingdoms, Khaz Algar, ...) lists the achievements of every zone on that continent, each once, and on the Azeroth world map those of every zone on it, instead of being greyed out (dev note: `KrowiAF_WorldMapButtonMixin:Refresh` unions the flat `addon.GetAchievementsInZone` lookup over the Zone-type descendants from `C_Map.GetMapChildrenInfo` when the map's `mapType` is Continent or World. The lookup itself is unchanged, so the Current Zone category, which never sits on a continent, is unaffected. The retired `Data/Retail/ExportedUiMaps.lua` fallback table and its loader are gone as well; every entry had been migrated into the `ZoneData` files)
