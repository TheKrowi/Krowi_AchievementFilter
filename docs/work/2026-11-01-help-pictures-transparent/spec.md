# Spec: Help pictures without a black box
Intent: [intent.md](intent.md). Status: accepted

## Requirements
1. Wherever a help picture shows the run's backdrop, it is transparent: the margin beside a frame's own edge, and the backdrop inside the cut that joins it (around a frame's corners, between the tabs, above the window beside the category list, beside the map).
2. The soft fade where a picture cuts through content stays as it is.
3. The frames stay as they are in game: opaque parts opaque, their dark borders too, and see-through parts (a tooltip's background, a menu's shadow) as see-through as in game, never tinted.
4. Each picture keeps its empty room beside a frame's edge and its place in the pane; `Options/Window/Help/Pictures.lua` is regenerated by the remake.
5. A menu's or tooltip's dark background is never taken for the backdrop: where the run cut one short (FilterMenu, TipEarnedBy, TipPoints), the side is a cut with its fade, not a straight line.
6. `Make-HelpPictures.ps1` makes every picture this way, from a `/kaftest pictures` run.

## Root cause
- **The black:** `Make-HelpPictures.ps1:371-373` clears each picture to black under its parts, and an edge side's `-Margin` (`:348-349`) is that black, faded by alpha only over its outer 40 px (`:385-401`). On the old pane the black passed for a shadow; the pane in the game's Options look (#379) is dark grey, about RGB 28, 30, 29 in Krowi's screenshot, so the black shows as a box.
- **One shot cannot tell black from a dark frame:** a frame's dark border, a menu's background and a tooltip's background over the black backdrop are as dark as the backdrop (measured in the files: the menu's background 0, the tooltip's 40 of 765). So the tool's side check (`Get-Side`, `:222-229`) took the bottoms the run cut short (`Tests/Pictures.lua:388`, `:430`, `:469`) for frame edges and `Get-Trimmed` (`:232-239`) moved the cut up to the last line of text, and any key on the black also takes the frames' dark borders (Krowi's look in game at the first build, below).
- **One pink shot alone is not enough either:** Retail's tooltip background is partly see-through (the 40 of 765 is darker than its colour 0.09, 0.09, 0.19 at full alpha, `Blizzard_SharedXML/SharedTooltipTemplates.lua:231-234`), and so is the menus' shadow, so over pink they come out tinted; working the pink out of them would also eat pink and purple text.
- **Reproduction:** Krowi's screenshot of the Categories help (2026-11-01), and `Media/Help/*.tga` at `18cce31e`.

## Design
- **Two shots per picture:** after the black shot the run turns its backdrop pink (1, 0, 1) and takes a second shot 3 s later, so its file has a second of its own; it records the time as `PinkTime` on the shot and turns the backdrop black again for the next step. When the game's tooltip came over the picture in between, the pink shot is skipped and named in the run's observations.
- **The tool works out the transparency:** the pink adds (1 - alpha) times (255, 0, 255) to the black shot, so per pixel alpha = 1 - (red difference + blue difference) / 510, and the colour is the black shot's over alpha. Green does not change; where it does, or red and blue disagree, the pixel moved between the shots (the reward preview's model, a reminder's timer) and is taken whole from the black shot. Alpha within 10 of 0 or 255 is snapped there. Both shots are cut and scaled alike before the step.
- **The side check reads the same transparency:** a line is backdrop where at most 0.5% of it is more than 10% opaque, so a menu's or tooltip's background is content and the side through it a cut, with no flag from the run. The rest of the side check is as in #376, with two limits the first run of 2026-11-01 showed the black shot had hidden: the reach for a frame's edge grows from 24 to 32 px (the tabs' shadow ends 25 px below the window's cut; the window's border beside the category list is 24 px, with a black line inside it that the black test took for the backdrop), and a frame over another one's corner keeps its edge where 80% of the line 3 px out is backdrop, not 85% (the window is 15.4% of the guide panel's top).
- **The pop-out at full opacity:** with Fade when not hovered on, the pop-out is see-through while the mouse is off it, which the pink shot shows; the run's pop-out step sets the pop-out it opened to full opacity before the shot, and the mouse's next enter or leave fades it again.
- **What moved since #376 and stays:** Summary, CompactList and Colors are cuts on the sides where #376 found black art lines in the window's border (they cut through the window now, with the fade). TipEarnedBy's top stays the tooltip's edge: with 42328 (Decision) the Earned By part is the tooltip's first line, as in #376.
- **The fades stay:** a cut side keeps its `-Fade` curve; an edge side's margin is transparent room.
- **The run is Krowi's:** one `/kaftest pictures` on Retail with the other addons off (as in #376), best with `/console screenshotFormat png` first: jpg blurs colour, which leaves a fringe where a frame meets the pink, and the tool warns about it. Mists keeps Retail's pictures.
- A picture without its pink shot (a run from before this change) is not made; its file stays.

## Options considered
1. **Chosen:** black and pink shot, the design above.
2. Pink shot only (Krowi's first proposal): dark borders stay, but see-through backgrounds and the menus' shadow come out tinted, or pink and purple text turns see-through.
3. The first build: a key on the black, flooding from the margins, the 19 files converted without a run. Built, deployed and turned down in game (Decision).
4. Drop the empty room beside each frame's edge. Turned down at the first gate.

## Areas of concern
- **Taint and secret values:** the run sets the colour of its own backdrop texture and reads `GameTooltipOver` once more, as before each shot; no new frame, hook or Blizzard API.
- **Clients:** the pictures are Retail's and show on every client the TOC lists (Retail and Mists; Krowi's call of 2026-10-30). Mists' options window is in the same look.
- **Blizzard API changes:** none.
- **Other addons, plugins and skins:** none; the pictures are textures in the addon's own window. The enlarged view's scrim (`Krowi_Options/Window.xml:200-202`, black at 0.75) now shows through the margins.
- **Saved variables:** `KrowiAF_DebugTable.Pictures` gains `PinkTime` per shot; an older run without it reads as before. No migration.
- **Localization, embedded libraries, data snapshots:** none.
- **Player-facing text:** none. 105.0 is not released and its changelog already says the pictures are new, so no changelog line.

## Verification
- No scenario: `pictures` is a game-only suite, and the headless `help` suite (each picture has its size and file) stays green, with `Check-Repo.ps1`.
- Before the run, the tool on a faked pair (two recorded black shots with pink twins) shows the pairing, the transparency, the side check and the files work.
- After Krowi's run: every picture looked at over the pane's grey and over magenta, the dark borders, the tooltips' backgrounds and the three cut bottoms zoomed in.
- In game on Retail and Mists: deploy, and Krowi's look at the help pane and the enlarged view.
- `krowi:taint-reviewer` on the `Tests/Pictures.lua` change, `/code-review` before the PR.
